The Challenge

Who should marketing target?

The client had a massive dump of historical sales data but no way to differentiate between a loyal repeat buyer and a one-time big spender. They needed a data-driven way to allocate marketing spend.

Execution

Data Pipeline

SQL: Clean & Aggregate → Python: Quintile Scoring → Power BI: Viz
  • Data Cleaning (SQL): Removed cancelled orders, negative quantities, and missing Customer IDs. Grouped sales at the invoice level.
  • RFM Calculation (Python/Pandas): Calculated days since last purchase (R), total invoices (F), and total spend (M) per customer.
  • Scoring: Assigned 1-5 scores using Pandas `qcut()` to distribute customers into logical percentiles.

Actionable Segments

The business can now pull dynamic lists of "At Risk" customers for win-back email campaigns, and exclude "Lost" customers from ad targeting, saving budget.

The Challenge

Why are people leaving?

HR directors needed a centralized view to monitor the company's attrition rate. They wanted to filter by department, job role, and salary bucket to find correlations between pay, performance, and turnover.

Execution

Modeling & DAX

  • Data Modeling: Built a Star Schema in Power BI, connecting fact tables (employee records) with dimension tables (departments, roles).
  • DAX Calculations: Wrote custom measures for `Attrition Rate %`, `YTD Terminations`, and dynamic conditional formatting based on targets.
  • UI Design: Created a dark-themed, intuitive layout prioritizing high-level KPIs before drilling down into demographic charts.
Outcome

Strategic HR Decisions

The dashboard revealed a spike in turnover among mid-level sales representatives. HR used this data to restructure commission brackets, improving retention in the subsequent quarter.
