Featured Case Study

Customer Behavior & RFM Analysis — Online Retail

View GitHub Repository ↗
01 · Business Problem

Every customer was treated the same

The company had thousands of customers but no way to tell them apart. Marketing budget was spread evenly instead of where it mattered, loyal customers got no special attention, and at-risk customers left without anyone noticing.

  • Which customers generate the highest revenue?
  • Which customers are likely to stop purchasing?
  • How should marketing campaigns differ between segments?

03 · Workflow

Raw data to boardroom-ready dashboard

Raw Data→ SQL Cleaning→ RFM Calculation→ Segmentation→ Business Insights→ Excel Dashboard
04 · SQL Analysis

Customer-level aggregation in SQL

Cleaned and validated over 540,000 line-level transactions, then built Recency, Frequency and Monetary metrics per customer using joins, CTEs, CASE WHEN logic, window functions and aggregations — producing one analytical table ready for segmentation.

05 · Segmentation

Seven behavior-based customer segments

Champions Loyal Customers Potential Loyalists New Customers About To Sleep At Risk Lost Customers
06 · Python EDA

Exploratory analysis with Pandas & Matplotlib

Used Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib) to explore purchasing patterns across the cleaned dataset and validate what the SQL segmentation was showing, before handing the results to the dashboard layer.

07 · Dashboard

Interactive Excel dashboard

Built to track Total Revenue, Total Customers, Total Orders, Average Order Value, Champions % and Revenue At Risk — the KPIs a retail business needs to monitor segment health week to week.

08 · Recommendations

What the business should do next

  • Protect revenue by prioritizing retention offers for the At Risk and About To Sleep segments before they churn.
  • Reward Champions and Loyal Customers with recognition and early access instead of generic blanket discounts.
  • Route new customers into a structured onboarding flow to convert them into Potential Loyalists faster.
