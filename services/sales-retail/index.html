Case Study

Enterprise Omnichannel Audit

A production-style retail audit focused on identifying margin leakage across promotions, returns, customer identity, and multi-channel transaction data.

Promo abuse detected in 1.50% of transactions.

Gross sales reached $6,976,624.76.
Realized margin reached $2,462,863.19.
Analytical Principle

Signals are for prioritization, not automatic conclusions.

Anomaly and leakage indicators should help teams decide where to investigate. They should not automatically be treated as confirmed fraud or booked financial loss.
